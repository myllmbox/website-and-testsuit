Produce the complete product catalog as ONE JSON object that follows the provided schema exactly:

  - "weight_kg": shipping weight
  - "dimensions": width, height and depth in centimetres
  - "tags": 3 to 5 lowercase search tags
  - "supplier": an invented supplier name and its country
  - "warranty_months": 0, 12, 24 or 36
  - "variants": 1 to 3 variants, each with its own sku suffix, a label (size, colour or pack size) and a price
- "summary": the total product count, the number of products per category, and the average price per category.

Write every product in full — no placeholders, no "...", no "and so on". Keep descriptions specific to each product.
